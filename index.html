Clinical Screening & Limitations Notice

This system analyzes relative plantar pressure patterns from 5 discrete Velostat sensor locations for preliminary biomechanical screening and academic research. It does not diagnose medical conditions. Conditions including flatfoot (pes planus), high arch (pes cavus), plantar fasciitis, metatarsalgia, and diabetic foot complications require clinical evaluation, radiographic imaging (e.g. weight-bearing X-rays for Meary's/calcaneal pitch angles), and podiatric examination. Where the 5-sensor layout is insufficient to differentiate subtle anatomical variants, outputs reflect pressure-pattern abnormalities rather than confirmed pathology.
